{{< intro >}}
FodyDev is an open-source organization. Our goal is to create free and open tools that support and promote African sovereignty.
{{< /intro >}}

We believe that African sovereignty and autonomy are important. Technology should help protect, empower, and support African communities.

By building tools in the open, we can work together and learn from each other. We believe that when people share ideas and work openly, they can create more, achieve self-reliance, and do better.

FodyDev is here to connect people, build useful projects, and promote African sovereignty through open source technology.
