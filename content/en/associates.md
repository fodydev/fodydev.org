FodyDev is made up of people who care about open source and African sovereignty. We also work with other groups who support our goals.

{{< /intro >}}

## Core Team

- **[Brady Fomegne](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brady-fomegne/)** – Founder and Lead Developer
  Brady created FodyDev and leads the development of projects like Afrim. He works to support African sovereignty using modern technology.

- **Contributors**
  Many developers, advocates, and translators help us improve our tools. Anyone can become a contributor by joining on GitHub.

## Partners

- **[Kasahorow](https://kasahorow.org)**
  They work to make African languages useful and modern. They support learning and using African languages in everyday life.

- **[OSS Cameroon](https://osscameroon.com)**
  They promote open source in Cameroon. They support developers, events, and projects that make technology more open and accessible.

We thank everyone who helps FodyDev grow.
